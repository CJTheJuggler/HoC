Our Mission

At House of Circus, our mission is to create a vibrant and inclusive community where individuals can explore and express their creativity through the art of circus. We are dedicated to providing a supportive and inspiring environment that fosters growth, learning, and collaboration among circus artists of all levels. Our goal is to promote the art of circus as a powerful form of expression, entertainment, and personal development, while also advocating for diversity, equity, and inclusion within the circus community.

Meet the Team

Jake

Performer, Co-Director


I’m Jake, I’ve been performing for over 25 years. I specialise in fire juggling, fire breathing and contact ball, combining technical skill with a high-energy performance style.
I’ve performed at festivals, club nights and private events across the UK and Europe, and what I enjoy most is creating memorable experiences for a crowd. For me, circus is about more than performing - it’s also about sharing skills, teaching others and inspiring people to get involved.
Based in Leicester, I regularly travel throughout the UK and Europe, bringing experience, energy and precision to every performance.
